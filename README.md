# Japanese Dream House — Yama no Ie

A small modern Japanese countryside home for two, published as an explorable
three.js walkthrough.

**Live:** https://egannguyen.github.io/japanese-dream-house/

## Explore

- **WASD / arrow keys** — move
- **Mouse** — look
- **E** — open/close doors and switch lights
- **V** — open the twelve-view architectural gallery
- **Esc** — return from a gallery view to the walkthrough

The floor-plan button in the gallery opens JDH-A101. The house is one coherent
model: all elevations, the bird's-eye view, the floor plan and the interiors are
generated from the same 8.00 × 8.50 m internal envelope.

## Specification summary

- Internal 8.00 × 8.50 m, external 8.36 × 8.86 m
- One storey, low 18° charcoal gable roof
- Warm cedar board-and-batten exterior, bronze frames, stone plinth
- Pale oak interior, warm-white plaster, vaulted timber ceiling
- Bedroom, open living/dining/kitchen, bathroom with ofuro, separate WC,
  laundry/storage, linen store and entrance hall
- South engawa, private Japanese garden, stone path, lantern and mature trees
- 12 fixed camera views, interactive doors and lights

Built with three.js. No neighbouring houses; the model is a visualisation study,
not a construction set.
